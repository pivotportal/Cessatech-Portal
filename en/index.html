About Cessatech

Cessatech is a Danish company developing hospital medicines for children. Its lead product, CT001, is a and nasal spray for moderate to severe acute pain in children aged 1 to 17.

In the US, manufacturer STAQ Pharma released the first commercial batch at the end of August 2026, and launch activities have started at selected hospitals. In the EU, the adopted a negative opinion in July 2026, and partner Proveca has requested a .

The company has three employees and works through partners. Besides CT001 there are three earlier programs: CT002, CT003 and CT004.

Risks to weigh

Facts from the company’s own documents, without our own assessments.

Funding and going concern

In the annual report 2025 the auditor points to a material uncertainty about going concern. Cash was DKK 2.3M on June 30, 2026. The DKK 10M loan facility is undrawn and conditional.
Source: Annual report 2025, pp. 18 and 31 ↗ · Q2 report 2026, p. 2 ↗

Regulatory in the EU

The CHMP has adopted a negative opinion on the PUMA application for CT001. EU approval requires the re-examination to change the outcome.
Source: PR Jul 24, 2026 ↗ · Q&A Aug 3, 2026 ↗

Commercial in the US

The company has no product revenue yet. The CEO writes that establishing CT001 in the market will take time.
Source: PR Aug 27, 2026 ↗

Partner dependence

Proveca files and is to hold the authorisation in the EU. In the US, Ventis Pharma sells and STAQ Pharma manufactures. Cessatech’s income flows through its partners.
Source: Annual report 2025, p. 9 ↗

Timelines

The US launch has moved several times: second half of 2024, then 2025, then Q1 2026. The first commercial batch was released in August 2026.
Source: Q2 report 2024, p. 3 ↗ · Q1 report 2026, p. 4 ↗ · PR Aug 27, 2026 ↗

Small organization

The company had three employees on average in 2025 and depends on a few key people and partners.
Source: Annual report 2025, p. 32 ↗
